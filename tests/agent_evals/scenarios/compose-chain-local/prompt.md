Build a small chain of three local data products under products/, all in domain payments, owned by team payments-data, with ids payments.payments_raw, payments.payments_clean and payments.merchant_daily_volume, each writing a parquet file. First, products/payments_raw/contract.fluid.yaml: a source-aligned product (SDP, Bronze) ingesting the CSV products/payments_raw/data/payments.csv (columns payment_id, merchant_id, paid_at, amount, currency), exposing the table payments_raw. Second, products/payments_clean/contract.fluid.yaml: an aggregated product (ADP, Silver) consuming payments_raw, removing the duplicate payment_ids and casting amount to decimal, exposing the table payments_clean with payment_id unique. Third, products/merchant_daily_volume/contract.fluid.yaml: a consumer-aligned product (CDP, Gold) consuming payments_clean, exposing the table merchant_daily_volume with merchant_id string, day date, total_amount decimal and payment_count integer. Run all three locally, daily.
